About this service

What is Under-House & Crawlspace Leak Repair?

Under-house and crawlspace leak repair focuses on locating and fixing water leaks within supply and drainage lines hidden underneath your home's living areas. Left unchecked, crawlspace moisture damages wood structures, fosters mold colonies, and causes floor sagging.

Naples' high coastal humidity and sandy sub-soils can accelerate the wear of underground plumbing joints and copper fittings. Our team uses non-destructive acoustic sensors and endoscopic inspection tools to isolate leaks without disturbing your finished floors, preserving your home's structural integrity.

How it works

How Does Under-House Leak Detection Work?

Our technicians conduct an initial perimeter survey and crawlspace moisture mapping to evaluate visible symptoms and isolate suspicious zones.

We run pressure decay tests and utilize sensitive acoustic listening discs to detect the ultrasonic sound of pressurized water escaping underneath your home.

Once the exact pipe breach is pinpointed, we review repair alternatives and provide clear, upfront pricing before any work begins.

Our certified plumbers access the crawlspace to replace defective fittings, solder clean joints, or install heavy-duty PEX bypasses meeting Florida plumbing codes.

We re-pressurize the lines and perform a final diagnostic check to ensure zero leaks remain, leaving your home safe, dry, and protected.
