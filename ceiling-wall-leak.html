What to Expect During Ceiling Wall Leak Repair

Our team conducts a thorough inspection to identify the source of the leak. This step typically takes about 30 minutes, ensuring we understand the problem fully.

Using advanced tools like plumbing snakes and water pressure gauges, we accurately locate the leak. This process is crucial for effective repair and can take 1-2 hours.

Once the leak is identified, we discuss the repair plan with you. This includes the scope of work, estimated time, and costs involved, ensuring transparency.

Our licensed plumbers execute the repair plan using high-quality materials like PVC pipe or copper pipe. This phase usually takes 2-4 hours, depending on the complexity of the leak.

After repairs, we conduct a final inspection to ensure everything is fixed correctly. We also clean up the work area, leaving your home as we found it.
